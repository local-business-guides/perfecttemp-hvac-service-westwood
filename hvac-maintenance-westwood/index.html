What to prepare and discuss

The details you share can help the technician understand your concerns. Before the appointment, make a short note of anything you’ve observed and when it began. You might include whether the issue happens all the time or only at certain times, and whether it affects one part of the home or more than one.

A useful preparation list:

  • Write down sounds, smells, or changes in operation that caught your attention.
  • Note any recent service or repair information you have available.
  • Make sure you can describe where the equipment is located.
  • List questions about the visit and any recommended follow-up.

Don’t try to open equipment, handle wiring, or make a repair just to prepare. If the system is behaving in a way that concerns you, leave the inspection to a professional. You can also ask what the technician observed before agreeing to any additional work.
