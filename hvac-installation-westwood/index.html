What to review before work begins

A planning visit or project discussion should establish what equipment and related work may be needed. Ask the contractor to consider the space being served and the existing heating and cooling arrangement. Don't assume that a system that fits one home will suit another, or that existing components can automatically stay in place.

Use this short checklist when you speak with a contractor:

  • Identify the rooms or areas the equipment should serve.
  • Describe any changes you expect to make to the space.
  • Share what you know about the current heating and cooling setup.
  • Ask what the installation plan includes and what remains undecided.
  • Confirm what you should do before the work is scheduled.

HVAC Installation Westwood should be discussed in terms of your property, not just an equipment label. If the proposal is unclear, ask for an explanation before agreeing to the work.
